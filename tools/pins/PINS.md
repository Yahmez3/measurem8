# MeasureMate Pinterest pins

Six pins, 1000 × 1500 px, in `pins/` at the site root (also live at
`https://measurem8.app/pins/<name>.png`). Every number matches the live site.
Re-render after any edit with `node render.js` in this folder.

## One-time setup (James)

1. Go to **business.pinterest.com** and create a free **business** account.
   Name it **MeasureMate**. Use the brand mark `brand/logo-mark.svg` (export a
   PNG) as the profile picture.
2. **Claim the website**: Settings, then Claimed accounts, then Website,
   enter `measurem8.app`, and choose the **HTML tag** option. Pinterest shows a
   line like `<meta name="p:domain_verify" content="...">`. Send that line to
   Claude, which adds it to the homepage and deploys. Then press Verify.
   Claiming adds your name and logo to every pin from the site and unlocks
   analytics.
3. Create one board:
   - **Name:** Baking Conversions & Kitchen Charts
   - **Description:** Free kitchen conversion charts: cups to grams, butter
     sticks, flour, oven temperatures, and US vs UK vs Australian measures.
     From MeasureMate, the free cooking converter at measurem8.app.

## Posting

Post **one pin every two or three days** rather than all six at once.
Pinterest rewards steady activity, and it gives Search Console a spread of
visits to show. For each pin, upload the image and paste the title,
description, link and alt text below.
