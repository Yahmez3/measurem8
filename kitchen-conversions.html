How we measure

Every kitchen figure on MeasureMate comes from a named source and uses the same conventions, so the charts, the pages and the cooking converter always agree. Cup weights assume a US recipe cup of 240 mL, the size modern American recipes and nutrition labels use, with dry ingredients spooned into the cup and levelled. Brown sugar is firmly packed, because recipes expect it to be. Butter is based on the US stick of exactly four ounces. Australian cups (250 mL) and tablespoons (20 mL) are scaled from the same figures.

The weights themselves come from King Arthur Baking's ingredient chart and the USDA's FoodData Central reference tables, with Australian sources such as the Australian Women's Weekly where local practice differs. Where sources disagree, the ingredient page says so and shows each figure. Every page lists its sources at the end and shows the date it was last updated.
